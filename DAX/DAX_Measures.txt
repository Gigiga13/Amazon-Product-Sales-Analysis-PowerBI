========================================
AMAZON PRODUCT SALES ANALYSIS - DAX
===================================

1. CALENDAR TABLE

---

Calendar Table =
CALENDAR(
MIN(Amazon_Data[Order Date]),
MAX(Amazon_Data[Order Date])
)

2. CALENDAR TABLE COLUMNS

---

Month =
FORMAT(
'Calendar Table'[Date],
"MMM"
)

Month Number =
MONTH(
'Calendar Table'[Date]
)

Quater Number =
QUARTER(
'Calendar Table'[Date]
)

Quater =
CONCATENATE(
"Qtr ",
'Calendar Table'[Quater Number]
)

Week =
WEEKNUM(
'Calendar Table'[Date]
)

3. YEAR-TO-DATE (YTD) MEASURES

---

YTD Sales =
TOTALYTD(
SUM(Amazon_Data[Price(Dollar)]),
'Calendar Table'[Date]
)

YTD Product Sold =
TOTALYTD(
COUNT(Amazon_Data[Product Category]),
'Calendar Table'[Date]
)

YTD Reviews =
TOTALYTD(
SUM(Amazon_Data[Number of reviews]),
'Calendar Table'[Date]
)

4. QUARTER-TO-DATE (QTD) MEASURE

---

QTD Sales =
TOTALQTD(
SUM(Amazon_Data[Price(Dollar)]),
'Calendar Table'[Date]
)

5. TOTAL SALES / PRICE

---

Total Sales =
SUM(
Amazon_Data[Price(Dollar)]
)

6. TOTAL REVIEWS

---

Total Reviews =
SUM(
Amazon_Data[Number of reviews]
)

7. TOTAL PRODUCTS SOLD

---

Total Product Sold =
COUNT(
Amazon_Data[Product Category]
)

8. TOP 5 PRODUCTS BY YTD SALES

---

Top 5 Products by YTD Sales are filtered using Power BI's built-in Top N filter.

Filter:
Product Name → Top N → Top 5 → By value: YTD Sales

9. TOP 5 PRODUCTS BY YTD REVIEWS

---

Top 5 Products by YTD Reviews are filtered using Power BI's built-in Top N filter.

Filter:
Product Name → Top N → Top 5 → By value: YTD Reviews
